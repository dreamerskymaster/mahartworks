About the artist

In her words

Life has taken me through many different roles—from being a working woman to embracing life as a homemaker. Somewhere along the way, I found something that brought me back to myself: art. What began as a simple hobby after COVID gradually became a journey of creativity, learning, and self-discovery.

I started with sketching and crayons, then explored sketch pens, and eventually discovered acrylics. With every new medium, I learned something different, experimented with new techniques, and slowly found my own way of expressing myself through art.

What began as a hobby soon became much more than that. Art became my stress buster, my escape, and my little space of peace. Every painting has been a new learning experience—sometimes through practice, sometimes through experimentation, and often through simply trying something new.

Six years into this journey, I continue to learn and explore every day. I believe there is always something new to discover in art, and that constant learning is what keeps my creativity alive.

For me, art is not just about the finished painting; it is about the journey of learning, experimenting, evolving, and finding joy in every stroke.

From life’s many roles to discovering my artist’s soul, every canvas reflects a part of my journey. 🎨❤️
