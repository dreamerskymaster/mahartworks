Certificate of Authenticity

Verify a certificate

Every original by Mahalakshmi Srikanth comes with a Certificate of Authenticity signed by her. Its number, in the form MS-YYYY-NNNN, is printed beside the QR code. Enter it to see the artwork it was issued for.

A certificate is genuine only if its number opens the same artwork that it describes. For any doubt, ask through her contact details or write to mahasriphoto24@gmail.com.
